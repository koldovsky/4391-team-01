Types of Cloud Computing:

Software as a Service (SaaS)

Read More SaaS is a method of utilization a network of remote servers hosted on the Internet for data storage, management, and process. Optimizer provides customers with software solutions by means of our own servers.

Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Read More PaaS is a special way of platform-based cloud computing which empowers customers to manage and develop their own software and applications without creating and maintaining the type of infrastructure necessary to develop and launch software.

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

Read More IaaS provides clients with data management infrastructure. Enterprises using IaaS maintain the majority of their own programs, apps, and processes, but integrate them into the provider’s infrastructure, which is controlled, supported, repaired, and updated by the provider.
